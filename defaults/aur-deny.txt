# ress — AUR packages never to build, one name per line.
#
# A vault records package names; a restore turns those names into PKGBUILDs
# fetched from aur.archlinux.org and run on this machine. Anything listed here
# is dropped from that list before the question is even asked, on every vault,
# including your own.
#
# Add your own in ~/.config/ress/aur-deny (same format). Nothing is listed by
# default: what belongs here is a judgement about a package, and that judgement
# is yours.
